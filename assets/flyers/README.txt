Drop the flyers into this folder with these exact filenames:

  tuesday-soirees-november.jpg (Tuesday Soirées — Nov, weekly card)
  live-music-november.jpg      (Live Music Wednesdays — Nov, weekly card)
  trivia-night.jpg             (Trivia Night)
  pau-hana.jpeg                (Pau Hana daily happy hour)
  girl-dinner.png              (Girl Dinner)
  tuesday-jazz-december.jpg    (December Jazz Nights — featured preview)
  live-music-december.jpg      (December Live Music — featured preview)

Each flyer is wired into its section by filename. Once a file lands
here, it appears immediately on the page. Until then, a striped
"flyer coming soon" placeholder shows where the flyer will go.
